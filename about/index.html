I build speech and audio systems that move from research ideas into reliable embedded products.

I graduated from East China Normal University with a master's degree in Computer Technology in 2015.

I have worked at uvoicetech for more than 10 years, with a focus on embedded-side audio processing.

My experience spans speech signal processing, noise reduction, music and film source separation, wake word detection, ASR, and speaker recognition. Beyond algorithm design, I also focus on the engineering path that brings models to real devices: training, inference optimization, embedded deployment, NPU porting, and fixed-point quantization on constrained platforms.

I am especially interested in making high-performance audio systems practical, stable, and efficient across heterogeneous hardware environments.

Core areas

  • Speech signal processing and audio enhancement
  • Noise reduction and source separation
  • Wake word detection, ASR, and speaker recognition
  • Training, inference, and deployment optimization
  • Embedded systems, NPU integration, and fixed-point quantization

Work style

  • Move from prototype to production with a strong systems mindset
  • Balance model quality, latency, memory, and hardware constraints
  • Translate algorithm ideas into deployable, maintainable implementations
